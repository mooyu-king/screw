How Screw Games work

Screw Games turn a familiar little object into a problem worth slowing down for. Screws hold a stack of plates together. Holes offer temporary space. Colors create groups. The satisfying part is seeing how one small move changes the whole board.

Start with Screw Puzzle if you enjoy planning an order: remove the fasteners from a plate, let it fall, and uncover what sits underneath. Screw Sort moves top nuts between upright rods until every stack holds one color. Screw Jam dismantles a colorful factory model, collecting groups of three before its shared buffer fills.

All six games run in your browser and begin with a forgiving warm-up. The second level adds dependencies and pressure without a timer forcing you to hurry. You can retry the same puzzle, compare approaches, or ask for a fresh layout. Screw Box adds sequential color orders, Screw 3D brings a rotating six-sided model, and Screw Escape turns support removal into a marble-routing challenge. Sign in before a new run to earn 2 Points for Level 1 or 10 for Level 2 across all six games.

A few things you might be wondering

What is a screw game?+

A screw game is a puzzle built around removing fasteners in the right order. Here you can move mounting screws, sort nuts on rods, or dismantle a model through color matches.

Can I play without an account?+

Yes. Level 1 in all six games is open to guests. Sign in to play Level 2 or use any helper. Guest practice progress stays in this browser; account Points require a verified signed-in run.

What makes Level 2 different?+

Level 2 adds layered dependencies and tighter working space. Layouts change when you start fresh, but Retry keeps the same arrangement so you can learn from a mistake.

Are the puzzles randomly generated?+

Yes. A seed creates each layout, and the rule engine checks a complete solution before the layout is shown. New Layout gives you another puzzle.

Can I play on my phone?+

The controls support tapping and keyboard input. Select a screw and an empty hole in Puzzle, a source and destination rod in Sort, or an exposed screw in Jam. The detailed Puzzle and Jam boards support panning on small screens.

Do I have to pay for another try?+

No. Retry and New Layout are free. This version does not sell moves or require an advertisement to restart.

What are Screw Points and badges?+

Signed-in verified clears earn 2 Points in Level 1 or 10 in Level 2. A mode badge requires a Level 2 clear. Browser practice records are kept separate from service-verified rewards.
