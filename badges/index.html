Skill leaves a mark

Points recognize participation and milestones. A badge recognizes a complete challenge. That difference is deliberate: an unfinished run can still teach you something and earn limited verified progress, but the badge waits for the full goal: release all plates, sort all rods, or clear the model.

The first collection has three badges, one for each playable mode. Future worlds will appear when they have real games and their own clear conditions.
