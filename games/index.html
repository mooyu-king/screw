Same workshop. Different ways to think.

Screw Puzzle asks you to plan a sequence of plate releases. Screw Sort asks you to organize top nuts into full, single-color rods. Screw Jam puts all your unfinished color groups in the same small tray. The modes share clear controls, but the useful decisions are different.

Every mode starts with Level 1. Box adds ordered containers, 3D adds spatial rotation, and Escape adds a marble route through ramps. You can also enter Level 2 directly when you want the challenge. A new layout is checked for a complete solution before it reaches the board, and Retry keeps the same seed for another approach.

Which kind of screw game do you enjoy?

Wooden support puzzles reward planning which plate to release next: try Screw Puzzle. Nut-and-bolt sorting is about uncovering buried colors without filling every spare rod: try Screw Sort. Color-collection games combine layered objects with limited tray space: try Screw Jam.

Other approaches to explore

For external examples, Nuts & Bolts: Screw Glass Puzzle on CrazyGames uses glass plates and color collection, while Get a Screw: 3D Puzzle! on CrazyGames adds rotation to find screws around an object. These links open another website; they are not playable modes in this collection.
