Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This policy describes screw.games and its game runtime. It was updated on October 27, 2026. Contact marlonqiu2026@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Features that are not enabled do not collect the corresponding account information.

Installed app and offline cache

The optional browser app uses a Service Worker to cache public pages, images and application assets on your device. Account APIs, sign-in callbacks and private responses are excluded. Clearing site data removes this cache. Reconnect to sign in, start verified runs or save Points. Installation help and installation outcomes may be recorded as aggregate events through the analytics service described below.

Browser storage

The site uses browser storage for navigation preferences, sound preferences, practice records and a recoverable game log. These records stay on the current browser unless a connected service receives a run for verification. Clearing site data removes local records and may reset preferences. Do not rely on browser practice as a permanent backup.

Connected game service

Google sign-in is provided through Supabase Auth. Its browser session identifies your account to our game service; the service verifies the access token before accessing account records. Guest practice is stored locally and does not earn account rewards. It receives the mode, seed, move sequence and run timing to validate points, badges and equipped rewards. Hosted services may also process IP addresses, request times, browser information and security logs. Verified progress cannot be recovered from local practice values alone.

Optional accounts

If Google sign-in is configured through Supabase, the identity provider processes the information necessary to authenticate you, such as an account identifier, email and basic profile information. We do not receive your Google password. Account configuration and progress linking must be enabled before cross-device saving is offered.

Operational diagnostics

We keep a restricted diagnostic log of sign-in reports, verified account access, game starts and settlements, check-ins, purchases and helper use. Records include timestamps, outcome codes, request identifiers, a pseudonymous account reference and actual Points or inventory changes. Browser reports are distinguished from server results. We do not include email addresses, passwords, access tokens or raw game actions in this log. It retains at most 1,000 recent records for up to 30 days; deleting your game profile removes linked diagnostic records. Anonymous reports cannot reliably be linked to an account. Diagnostic delivery may fail and is not a complete financial ledger. Only configured administrators can read these records.

Traffic analytics

We load https://data.1back.link/js/app.js and send analytics requests to https://data.1back.link/api/send to understand visits and page use. The main site and same-domain game runtime are identified as screw.games. If games are hosted on a separate subdomain, their analytics use that game hostname. Requests may contain ordinary network and browser information, page context and referrer information. We do not claim that these requests are anonymous or that no technical data is processed.

Sharing, retention and choices

Challenge links can disclose a game seed and selected result text to anyone you send them to. If you claim a share reward, the service records the platform you chose and your confirmation, but does not independently verify publication. Opening a social platform is optional and is subject to that platform’s policies. Purchase request identifiers and item-use logs prevent duplicate charges and reward claims. Do not include personal details in shared text. You can clear browser data, avoid optional sign-in, or contact us to request access or deletion of service records. Verification run records are designed for short retention; reward history persists while the corresponding profile is maintained. Operational retention depends on the deployed hosting configuration.

Children and changes

The puzzles are intended for a general audience, and we do not knowingly request sensitive information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us about data submitted by a child. This policy may change with the product; material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
