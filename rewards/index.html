Daily login rewards

Signed-in accounts receive 10 Points on their first visit each UTC day. Consecutive days 3, 7, 14 and 30 add 20, 50, 100 and 200 bonus Points: 670 Points over a complete 30-day cycle. The cycle repeats while your total streak continues. Missing a day restarts the streak, but keeps your earned assets. Login rewards are separate from game clear rewards and require the connected account service.

How verified points work

The game service replays your moves before awarding Points. Each completed Level 1 run earns 2 Points; each completed Level 2 run earns 10. Helpers do not reduce the clear reward. Failed runs earn no clear Points, and milestones do not add extra Points.

All six games use the same account balance. Retry is free. Each run can be rewarded only once, even if saving is retried after a connection failure. Sign in before starting a fresh run; guest practice cannot be converted into verified Points.

Spendable balance and permanent ownership

Earned Points increase both your lifetime total and spendable balance. Buying an item reduces only the balance. Purchased backgrounds and screw finishes remain yours; equip them independently in My Workshop and the current games. Cosmetic finishes keep sorting colors and symbols intact.

Helpers and sharing

Helper inventory carries over between days. Hint highlights a verified continuation, Undo restores the previous move, and Rescue adds temporary space in Level 2. Hint costs 2 Points, Undo costs 2 and Rescue costs 5. New accounts receive one of each. Inventory carries over, with each type limited to once per run. A run completed with helpers still earns its clear badge and first-clear achievement; a perfect run requires no helpers.

After using all three helpers, the configured share reward offers one Rescue for your inventory, usable in a later run. Sharing is optional and Retry stays free. The site records your confirmation of sharing, not independent verification that a social post was published. Purchases, inventory and verified rewards require the configured game service. Local practice does not award verified Points.
