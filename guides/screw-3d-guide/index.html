Screw 3D: a practical strategy guide

A useful screw may be on the side you have not looked at yet. Rotate a layered mechanical cube, inspect its six faces and dismantle its panels while matching the open color orders.

Open the practice board →

1. Search the hidden faces first

When the front offers no requested color, rotate before using temporary storage. A matching screw on the back costs no buffer space.

2. Separate camera and puzzle decisions

Reset view only resets orientation. Use it freely when lost; reserve Retry for restarting the actual screw sequence.

3. Compare unfinished panels

A panel with one screw remaining may expose a useful layer, but a different face may finish an order immediately. Read the boxes before choosing which benefit matters.

Try this exercise

Leave the visible screws untouched and make one slow tour of the object. Find a matching color on a side face, turn that face toward you, and collect it. Then reset the camera. Check that only the camera changed: the screw stays removed and the box keeps its progress. This separates an inspection action from a puzzle commitment.

A useful review after a loss

Keep the layout unchanged for one more attempt. Identify the first decision whose purpose you could not explain, then change that decision and observe the consequence. Do not compare only the move count: compare accessible choices and the remaining objective. New layout is useful for variety, while Retry is useful for learning a particular dependency. A shared seed lets somebody else investigate the same starting arrangement without copying your saved moves.

What a helper can and cannot establish

A Hint is offered only after finding a complete continuation within its search budget. An unsuccessful search does not prove that every route is blocked. Undo restores the preceding action once; it does not erase an arbitrary earlier mistake. Read the Rescue explanation for this mode before confirming, because collection games add space while Escape rewinds a support. Practice helpers are separate from purchased inventory in these new modes.

Common questions

Is this a rotating model or a flat board?+

The model uses three-dimensional face and screw coordinates projected into the view. Rotation changes the visible faces and the available click targets.

How do I see the back?+

Drag horizontally or use the rotation buttons. Tilt changes the vertical viewing angle so you can inspect the top and underside.

Can dragging remove a screw accidentally?+

The controls distinguish a drag from a tap using a movement threshold. A completed drag does not also collect its starting screw.

Does Reset view restart the level?+

No. It only restores the camera angle and zoom. Removed screws, orders and temporary slots stay unchanged.

Why is the highlighted Hint not visible?+

Its screw may be on another face. Rotate the object to locate the highlighted target.
