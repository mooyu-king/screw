Screw Escape: a practical strategy guide

Do not dismantle everything. Remove the right supports to tilt a chain of ramps, carry a marble through every brass key and reach the exit without entering a blocked chute.

Open the practice board →

1. Trace backward from brass keys

Identify the neighboring cells above the next key. Eliminate approaches that cross red chutes, then connect a surviving approach to the marble.

2. Only commit the route you need

Unused ramps do not need to be removed. Leaving uncertain cells untouched preserves choices until you understand the next checkpoint.

3. Understand the rewind

Undo and Rescue restore the preceding support action. If you changed an irrelevant ramp after an earlier mistake, rewinding that last action will not fix the earlier route.

Try this exercise

Start at the first brass key. Identify the columns that can reach it from the preceding row, then work backward toward the marble. Choose a support only after that connection is clear. If the marble stops at an undecided ramp, use the pause to inspect the next checkpoint rather than setting every nearby ramp.

A useful review after a loss

Keep the layout unchanged for one more attempt. Identify the first decision whose purpose you could not explain, then change that decision and observe the consequence. Do not compare only the move count: compare accessible choices and the remaining objective. New layout is useful for variety, while Retry is useful for learning a particular dependency. A shared seed lets somebody else investigate the same starting arrangement without copying your saved moves.

What a helper can and cannot establish

A Hint is offered only after finding a complete continuation within its search budget. An unsuccessful search does not prove that every route is blocked. Undo restores the preceding action once; it does not erase an arbitrary earlier mistake. Read the Rescue explanation for this mode before confirming, because collection games add space while Escape rewinds a support. Practice helpers are separate from purchased inventory in these new modes.

Common questions

Do I need to remove every screw?+

No. Build a route through every key to the exit. Ramps outside that route may remain untouched.

Which support sends the marble right?+

Remove the right support. Removing the left support creates a leftward slope. The arrow shows the committed direction.

What happens at an undecided ramp?+

The marble waits. There is no countdown or requirement to click before it falls.

What causes a loss?+

Leaving the grid, arriving at a red chute, missing a required key or reaching the bottom away from the exit ends the run.

Can I prepare ramps farther down?+

Yes. A later ramp can be set before the marble arrives. Make sure it belongs to the route you intend to use.
